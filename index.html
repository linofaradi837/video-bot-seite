video-bot

A personal tool for uploading my own videos to my own YouTube channel.

video-bot is a small private program that I use myself. It creates short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) and uploads them to my own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Creates short quiz and culture videos on my own computer.
  • Uploads each finished video to my own YouTube channel, with a title and description.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service or app. Nobody except me signs in or uses it.
  • It does not read, change or delete anything on a YouTube account other than uploading videos that I made.
  • It does not collect or share data about any other people.
